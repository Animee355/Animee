# Evangelion's New Kansai Travel Plan Story Is Coming to the Tokaido Shinkansen

*ANIMEE News Desk · October 10, 2026*

Fans of **Neon Genesis Evangelion** will have a new, unusual way to experience the franchise: an original voice drama called **Kansai Travel Plan**, planned as part of a JR Central collaboration for passengers travelling on Japan's Tokaido Shinkansen.

According to recent reports, the campaign is scheduled to run from **October 18, 2026, through January 31, 2027**. The story follows Misato Katsuragi and Ritsuko Akagi as they scout locations while planning a school trip for the Eva pilots. Kotono Mitsuishi and Yuriko Yamaguchi are reported to reprise their roles as Misato and Ritsuko.

## How can fans access the story?

The experience is designed for eligible passengers on the Tokaido Shinkansen rather than as a standard anime episode or a generally available streaming release. Reports say participants will need to open the campaign page while travelling and enable location and speed checks. Follow the current instructions on JR Central's campaign page, as eligibility and access details can change.

The collaboration is also connected with the Osaka presentation of the **ALL OF EVANGELION** 30th-anniversary exhibition. Reports describe a campaign ride certificate and an acrylic keychain available through designated redemption locations in Osaka, subject to the campaign's rules and availability.

## Is this a new Evangelion anime episode?

No report reviewed for this draft identifies *Kansai Travel Plan* as a new television episode or film. It is described as an original voice drama made for the travel collaboration. Fans should check the official campaign details for the exact content and access conditions.

## What should fans watch for next?

The campaign is an example of anime franchises expanding through location-based experiences, exhibitions, and limited audio content. ANIMEE will update this story if the organizers announce additional access details or changes to the schedule.
